Identification Rules

These rules decide which Oracle POs count as electricity POs. They are stored in the application database, not in code. Excluded POs are kept (marked, with the reason) so a rule change never loses data; after a change the stored POs are re-evaluated at once, and bills of excluded POs drop out of the dashboards.
