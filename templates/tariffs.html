Tariff-rule registry

No rule is built in. Enter a rule from the electricity board's tariff order, give its source, and approve it. Only APPROVED rules are used — and only for bills whose billing period they fully cover.

Without an approved rule the dashboards still show every charge printed on the bills, but an independent expected penalty, a contract-demand “violation” and a tolerance check are shown as unavailable. Hypothetical savings (kVAh) use the bill's own average rate unless a rule gives the marginal rate.
